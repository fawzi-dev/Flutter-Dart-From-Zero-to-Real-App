# Module 8 Assignment — Music Player Screen

**Independent practice (Level 2).** Turn a sketch into a widget tree — the real Module 8 skill.

## Brief
Sketch, then build, a static "now playing" screen: album art up top, track title + artist, a progress bar row (current time · a bar that fills the middle · total time), and a row of playback controls (previous · play · next).

## Requirements
1. **Sketch on paper first**, then build to it. Keep the sketch.
2. The progress row uses **fixed** time labels on the ends and an **`Expanded`** bar in the middle — verify by rotating.
3. Controls evenly spaced with `MainAxisAlignment`.
4. No hardcoded widths on anything that should flex; no overflow on rotation.

## Rubric
- [ ] A paper sketch exists and roughly matches your tree
- [ ] The middle bar flexes; the end labels don't
- [ ] Survives rotation with no stripes
- [ ] Nesting no deeper than it needs to be

## Difficulty
**Level 2 — Independent.** Extension (**Level 3**): add a Stack-based "badge" (e.g. a small heart) on the album art corner.

⏱️ Minimum 50 minutes.
